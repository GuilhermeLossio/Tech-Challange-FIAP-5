Safety

Secure simulation only

ECloe Pay validates a deterministic confirmation step, locks duplicate payment attempts with idempotency, and records only synthetic evidence.

Storage

Private Pay artifacts

Demo receipts and Pay evidence use a dedicated private Azure Blob container: ecloe-pay-demo-artifacts.

PostgreSQL

Pay-owned schema

Simulated sessions, payment orders, benefit interactions, and outbox rows belong to the dedicated ecloe_pay schema.

No real financial service is provided

ECloe Pay does not create real users, process real money, move funds, authorize payments, approve credit, perform fraud checks, open accounts, or provide regulated payment services.
